## Common questions

**Does the game cost anything after I download it?**
No. There are no in-app purchases and no ads. All the money in the game is pretend.

**Do I need an internet connection?**
No. The game plays fully offline.

**Do I need an account?**
No. There is nothing to sign up for.

**Where is my game saved?**
On your iPhone, inside the app. It saves as you play, so you can close the app at any time and
pick up where you left off. Your personal bests are kept on your phone too.

**Will an update keep my game?**
Usually. When an update changes how the game plays, a game in progress can't carry on under the new
rules, and you start a new one. Your personal bests are always kept.

**Can I move my game to another phone?**
Not yet. Your game lives on the phone you played it on. It may come across if you restore that
phone from an iCloud or computer backup.

**How do I start over?**
Tap **Main menu** at the foot of the Desk to get back to the opening screen, then start a new game
there.

**How do I turn the sound off?**
Tap **Sound** at the top right of the map.

**Does it work on iPad?**
Protection Co is made for iPhone. It runs on an iPad in an iPhone-sized window.

**What do I need to run it?**
An iPhone with iOS 15 or later.

## Tips for new players

- **Watch the combined ratio.** It's claims and costs over premium. Under 100% means you are making
  money on insurance.
- **Watch your capital.** Every policy you sell raises how much capital you need. Below 1.0 times
  what's required your rating falls to C, and four quarters at C ends the game. Growing too fast
  can sink a profitable company.
- **Don't chase luck.** Claims swing from quarter to quarter. The results tell you how much of a
  quarter was luck: a lucky quarter is no reason to cut your price, and an unlucky one is no reason
  to panic.
- **Hire before you're full.** Applicants arrive the quarter after you advertise, so post the job
  before your staff run out of room.
- **Learn who your customers are.** Some types of driver cost far more than others, and nobody
  tells you which. Your own claims show you, given time.
- **Start with stage 1.** Stage 2 is very hard on its own. Winning stage 1 and carrying that
  company on is the easier way in.
