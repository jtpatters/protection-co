*Effective October 18, 2026*

This policy covers the Protection Co app for iPhone.

## The short version

Protection Co does not collect, store or share any personal information. Nothing you do in the game
leaves your phone.

## What the app keeps, and where

The app keeps two things, both on your iPhone, inside the app's own storage:

- **Your game in progress**, so you can pick up where you left off.
- **Your personal bests**, such as your best quarter.

Neither is sent to us or to anyone else. Neither contains your name, contact details, location or
anything else about you, only how your pretend company is doing.

Deleting the app deletes both. Like any app's data, they may be included in your iPhone's own
backups to iCloud or your computer. Those backups are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy,
not by us.

## What the app does not do

- It does not make any internet connections.
- It has no accounts, sign-in or sign-up.
- It has no ads and no in-app purchases.
- It has no analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind, and does not use your device's
  advertising identifier.
- It does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or any other permission.
- It does not share or sell data to anyone, because it has no data to share.

## Children

Protection Co is not directed at children under 13. Since the app collects no personal
information from anyone, it collects none from children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If
a future version of the app ever collects any information, this policy will be updated before that
version is released.
