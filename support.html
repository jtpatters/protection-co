Common questions

Does the game cost anything after I download it?

No. There are no in-app purchases and no ads. All the money in the game is pretend.

Do I need an internet connection or an account?

No to both. The game plays fully offline, and there is nothing to sign up for.

Where is my game saved?

On your iPhone, inside the app. It saves as you play, so you can close the app at any time and pick up where you left off. Your personal bests are kept on your phone too.

Will an update keep my game?

Usually. When an update changes how the game plays, a game in progress can't carry on under the new rules, and you start a new one. Your personal bests are always kept.

Can I move my game to another phone?

Not yet. Your game lives on the phone you played it on. It may come across if you restore that phone from an iCloud or computer backup.

How do I start over?

Tap Main menu at the foot of the Desk to get back to the opening screen, then start a new game there.

How do I turn the sound off?

Tap Sound at the top right of the map.

Does it work on iPad?

Protection Co is made for iPhone only. It needs iOS 15 or later.

What to change: tips for new players

  • Watch the combined ratioIt's claims and costs over premium. Under 100% means you are making money on insurance.
  • Watch your capitalEvery policy you sell raises how much capital you need. Below 1.0 times what's required your rating falls to C, and four quarters at C ends the game. Growing too fast can sink a profitable company.
  • Don't chase luckClaims swing from quarter to quarter, and the results tell you how much of a quarter was luck. A lucky quarter is no reason to cut your price, and an unlucky one is no reason to panic.
  • Hire before you're fullApplicants arrive the quarter after you advertise, so post the job before your staff run out of room.
  • Learn who your customers areSome types of driver cost far more than others, and nobody tells you which. Your own claims show you, given time.
  • Start with stage 1Stage 2 is very hard on its own. Winning stage 1 and carrying that company on is the easier way in.
