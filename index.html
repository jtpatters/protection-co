A business game for iPhone

Start in Millbrook with an empty office, $400,000 and nine rivals. Every driver in town wants a price. Set yours too low and the claims will sink you; set it too high and nobody signs. Somewhere in between is a business.

What you do

  • Hire your peopleAdvertise the job, interview the applicants and make an offer. Every agent has their own skills, pay and mood. Train them, give them a raise, or let them go.
  • Price the riskSafe veterans, families, commuters, young drivers and drivers with a record all cost something different to insure, and nobody tells you what. Your own claims show you, given time.
  • Answer what lands on your deskA body shop offers a quiet arrangement. A car dealer wants a referral deal. More than fifty situations, each with several answers and consequences that can follow you for years.
  • Read the resultsEvery quarter ends with where the money went, what to change, and the town paper's take on you and your rivals.
  • Build a head officeOpen offices in Harborview, Cedar Falls and Fort Dunmore, then choose your head office's building and add departments one by one, from HR to a trading floor.
  • Ride out your luckClaims swing from quarter to quarter, and storms come with a warning. The game tells you how much of a quarter was luck, so you know when to hold your nerve.

Three stages

Stage 1 · The agency

Start here

One town, nine rivals, $400,000 and an empty office. Hire agents, set one price for every driver, and survive a bad quarter of claims as a small insurer.

To win: make a profit two calendar years in a row.

Stage 2 · The contender

Very hard

Less money and far more to decide: a price for each type of driver, up to three offices, marketing channels, storm cover, research and shares in your rivals. Storms arrive, and rivals fight back once you are big enough to notice.

To win: become one of the top three carriers in Millbrook with at least 1,500 policies, rated A, and hold it for four quarters in a row.

Stage 3 · The enterprise

Nearly impossible

A whole region to grow into: three more cities, a manager for each, and a head office whose departments you build. Very few companies started here last.

To win: an enterprise score of 85 out of 100 while rated A, earned on scale, reach, people and discipline.
